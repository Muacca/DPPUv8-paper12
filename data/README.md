# Archived calculations

- [sphere.json](sphere.json) gives the bounds on harmonic duration D, common squared radius r, K=24 finite count and energy intervals and 551 individual magnetic pairs, followed by uniform high-mode estimates. Each positive magnetic label represents 8k copies in a one-direction particle count. Both particle and hole energy contributions multiply that count by 4k in units of inverse endpoint radius.
- [nil_paired.json](nil_paired.json) tabulates two-band probabilities and selected partial sums on two time intervals, for two charge magnitudes and two real Holst parameters. These direct integrations check the equations and examples; the divergent/convergent all-mode claims have proofs in the manuscript.
- [third_order.json](third_order.json) stores the 991 mode-pair enclosures and tail bounds at the higher cutoff, including same-cutoff ratios between orders two and three. The turn-on of a lowest-shell contribution explains the increase of finite totals.

The output arrays distinguish one-direction expected number and two-direction excitation energy. All scripts use paths relative to this standalone repository.
